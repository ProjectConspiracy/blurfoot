The bonus, loss, speed, and interaction checks used targeted scene placement followed by real movement, E holds, and photograph timers. They verify those interactions and endings, but do not substitute for an uninterrupted human all-camera run. Browser audio state was checked; subjective sound quality was not evaluated.

## Decision and remaining work

Water no longer blocks the journey, and the three deliberate crossings preserve dry-land speed. Cover is now reflected in the drawn cones. No sight, photograph, or hazard-density tuning was needed to fix these changes.

This is a suitable point to establish the first custom Bigfoot asset's visual direction and silhouette, keeping the current 30×42 collision body. A human feel check remains useful before broad environment replacement or website release: judge whether 35% wading feels deliberate, whether the crossings read clearly, and whether the exposure tradeoffs feel fair. The circular water footprint has radius 15; it is separate from the rectangular solid-collision body and the 20-pixel photo silhouette radius.

The browser harness and screenshots used here are local QA artifacts, not a committed end-to-end suite. Website publication remains a separate task.
